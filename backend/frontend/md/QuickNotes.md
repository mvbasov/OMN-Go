##### 2026-08-03 12:00:00
**This page keeps your quick notes. Every new note comes to the top.**

To write a note, press the page title to expand the header, then press <i class="material-icons">insert_comment</i>. Write the text and press *Save*.

On Android you have two more ways:

* Press the *OMN-Go Quick Note* icon in the list of applications.
* Share text from another application to OMN-Go. Text that has no link comes here.

On a desktop, make a browser bookmark of this address and open it when you want to write a note. Use the port of your server:

`http://localhost:8080/Welcome.html?quicknote=1`

To remove this note, press <i class="material-icons">edit</i> and delete the text.
