##### 2026-08-03 12:00:00
**Your quick notes stay on this page. The newest note is at the top.**

On Android you write a note in two ways:

* Press the *OMN-Go Quick Note* icon. OMN-Go opens with the note box open.
* Share a text from a different application to OMN-Go. A text without a link comes to this page.

On each device you can also use the button. Press the title of the page. Then press <i class="material-icons">insert_comment</i>, write the text and press *Save*.

More ways to write a note: [User Manual](UserManual#quick-notes).
